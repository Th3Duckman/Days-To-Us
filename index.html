What’s built

Start with a private invite. Once you’re paired, every shared date and message lives in one place.

A space for two

Sign in with email and pair with a private, single-use invite that expires after 24 hours.

Dates that stay in sync

Save your anniversary and next visit together. See how many days you’ve shared and how many are left until you meet again.

Private chat

Share encrypted text, photos, and voice messages in your private conversation. Keep your shared recovery code safe.

Distance on demand

When both partners opt in and have recent readings, the map shows how many kilometers apart they are. Browser sharing runs while the app is open.

Control over sharing

Stop sharing location, leave your shared space, or delete your account from the app.

Save it to your Home Screen

Open the beta in Safari and add a Home Screen icon. The native builds have separate widgets for Days Together, Next Visit, and Distance.
