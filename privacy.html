Days to Us

Privacy and browser storage

This notice covers the Days to Us website and invite-only beta. Contact the Days to Us project team at yrntotstmmy2d7s@gmail.com about privacy, access to your data, or correction and deletion requests.

What the beta uses

Your email and sign-in information let us provide and secure your account. Dates, calendar plans, and days you mark free are stored with your shared space so both partners can see them. You can change the days you mark free in Calendar. When you enable secure chat, new message text is encrypted on your devices with a shared private recovery code before it reaches our server. Earlier messages remain unencrypted. Participants, timestamps, replies, receipts, reactions, and typing activity remain visible to the service. Your recovery code is kept on your devices and must be shared with your partner through a trusted private channel. Cloudflare hosts the beta app, and Supabase provides account and data services. This static roadmap can be served by Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages.

Location sharing

Location sharing starts off. When you turn it on, Days to Us receives your coordinates over an encrypted connection and shows recent readings to your paired partner. Server operators retain access to these coordinates. Distance requires recent readings from both phones. Tap Stop sharing in Map to turn it off. The optional street map stays off until you select it and sends tile requests for the area viewed and your IP address to OpenStreetMap.

Storage on your device

The beta stores your sign-in session, sample dates and free days when you use the local demo, and the sharing choice for your current browser tab. Secure chat keeps its recovery code in this tab’s session storage on the web and in protected device storage on native apps. If both partners lose the code and all unlocked devices, encrypted messages become unrecoverable. This website and the beta remember the appearance you choose. The site does not load advertising or analytics trackers. You can clear stored information using your browser’s site-data controls. Clearing the beta’s data signs you out of that browser.

Leaving and deleting your account

You can leave your shared space in Account. You can also delete your account there. Both actions clear the shared dates, calendar plans, free days, messages, and locations for that pair from the active app. Your partner keeps their own account.
